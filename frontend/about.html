What is this Website?

This is a digital syllabus management platform built for Vikrant University. It allows students to easily access, view, and download B.Tech course syllabi and study materials online.

Key Features

  • All B.Tech course syllabi in one place
  • Smart search to find content quickly
  • PDF preview before downloading
  • Easy download with one click
  • Mobile-friendly design
  • Organized by semester and subject

Available Courses

  • B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering
  • B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & ML
  • B.Tech Cyber Security
  • B.Tech Mechanical Engineering
  • B.Tech Civil Engineering
  • B.Tech Electrical Engineering
  • B.Tech Electronics & Communication

How It Works

  • Browse all available B.Tech courses
  • Click on any course to view its syllabus
  • Filter by semester or search by subject
  • Preview PDF directly in your browser
  • Download with one click

Why This Portal?

This portal was created to simplify access to academic resources and empower students with a centralized, easy-to-use platform.
